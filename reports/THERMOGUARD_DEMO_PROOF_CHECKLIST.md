## Mandatory Demo Rules for Presenters

1. **Be Scientifically Honest**: Always describe ThermoGuard as a **Decision Support & Investigation Prioritization** tool.
2. **Never Claim AI Fire Classification**: Remind judges that until human-validated ground-truth labels are acquired (per Phase VI protocol), claiming ML classification accuracy is scientifically prohibited.
3. **Emphasize Determinism**: Highlight that the risk engine is 100% reproducible and auditable—not a black box.
4. **Highlight Evidence Confidence**: Show that ThermoGuard is the only platform that separates the strength of physical evidence (Risk Score) from observational data quality (Evidence Confidence).
